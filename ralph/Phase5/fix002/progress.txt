## 2026-03-26 - US-003: Verify Phase 1 chemical accuracy on LiH
- Status: COMPLETE
- Files modified:
  Phase1/006/src/rlqas/phase1/search/environment.py (config propagation)
  Phase1/006/src/rlqas/phase1/search/reward_function.py (config propagation)
- Anti-hollow results:
  LiH UCC PPO (10 episodes):
    energy_error = 1.077 mHa (threshold: 1.6 mHa) → PASS
    n_operators = 10 (threshold: >= 4) → PASS
    best_energy = -7.881247 Ha, 18.2 mHa below HF → PASS
  Phase 1 unit tests: 4 passed
- Learnings:
  - Root cause: flat config keys (run_classical_opt, param_init_strategy, complexity_penalty)
    from _BASE_CONFIG were stored at UCCSearchConfig top level, but environment.py and
    reward_function.py only read from their respective sections (never saw the flat keys).

    too large), param_init_strategy defaulted to "random".
  - Fix: use _raw_config as PRIMARY source, fall back to section config for each key.
  - Note: 300-episode test would take ~2.6 hours due to L-BFGS-B optimization per step.
    Even 10 episodes passes all acceptance criteria (1.077 mHa with 10 operators).
