## Implementation Details (To be completed)
- **UCCSearchEnv**: Gym-compatible environment for UCC architecture search. State includes circuit architecture, parameters, and energy. Actions correspond to excitation operators.
- **UCCCircuitBuilder**: Builds UCC quantum circuits from excitation sequences. Supports single and double excitations.
- **UCCRewardFunction**: Computes rewards based on energy improvement with complexity penalty.
- **UCCSearchController**: Integrates environment, agent, and simulator for complete search process.
- **Configuration management**: Centralized configuration with validation and defaults from RLQAS specification section 3.4.
- **Integration testing**: End-to-end test with H2 molecule using Tasks 001, 002, 003 modules.
